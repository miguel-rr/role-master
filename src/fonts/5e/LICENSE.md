# Solbera D&D fonts (remakes)

Bookinsanity, Scaly Sans, Scaly Sans Caps, Mr Eaves Small Caps, Nodesto Caps
Condensed/Wide and Solbera Imitation are free remakes of the typefaces used in
the D&D 5th edition books, by Solbera and contributors, licensed
CC BY-SA 4.0. Source: https://github.com/jonathonf/solbera-dnd-fonts (woff2
builds taken from https://github.com/naturalcrit/homebrewery/tree/master/themes/fonts/5e).
